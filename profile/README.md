## Map the organization once, and you know who to train and what to build.

| | |
|---|---|
| **01 Map · People** | **Start with a real org chart.** Our own AI workflows draw your organization as it actually runs, inside your infrastructure, so your data stays home and nobody loses a week to interviews. Then we rank the gap roles standing between you and your objectives. |
| **02 Map · Process** | **Evaluate every department's workflows.** We score every workflow in every department with our own framework: what to automate, and in what order. |
| **03 Map · Technology** | **Uncover your real tech footprint.** The interdependencies between your systems decide which workflows can be automated at all. We chart them before promising anything. |
| **04 Train** | **Train your people.** We use AI to make your team more productive, never to replace it. A capability map for every employee shows where AI can carry part of the load, and where training lifts their fluency. |
| **05 Build** | **Build agentic workflows.** We build the best-scoring workflows inside your own infrastructure. Each one reads only what it is allowed to read, checks the answer against your own catalogue, and waits for a named person before anything moves. |
